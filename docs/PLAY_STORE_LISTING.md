🥗 Stop throwing away spoiled groceries and start saving money! Home Pantry is your smart, privacy-first kitchen companion designed to eliminate food waste, track product expiration dates, and suggest mouthwatering recipes using ingredients already in your pantry.

Whether managing daily dairy, fresh vegetables, dry spices, medicines, or cooking oils, Home Pantry keeps your household organized with automated alerts and intuitive inventory controls.

📦 UNIVERSAL PANTRY & GROCERY TRACKER
• Log leftovers, packaged food, produce, spices, medicines, and household essentials in seconds.
• Record purchase prices (₹), quantities, units, and storage locations (Fridge, Freezer, Pantry Cabinet, Medicine Box).
• Real-time Freshness Engine automatically calculates whether an item is Fresh, Expiring Soon, Expired, or Consumed.

⏱️ SMART EXPIRY ALERTS & TIMELY NOTIFICATIONS
• Receive scheduled daily reminders before your groceries reach their expiration date.
• Customize warning thresholds (1, 2, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days before expiry).
• Never forget that unopened yogurt or fresh herbs hiding in the back of the fridge.

📷 RAPID BARCODE SCANNER
• Point your camera to scan standard EAN/UPC product barcodes.
• Instant on-device lookup with offline catalog presets for lightning-fast inventory entry.

🍳 SMART RECIPES & COOKING INSPIRATION
• Discover delicious recipes matching your available pantry ingredients.
• Filter by difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), prep time, and dietary categories.
• Includes step-by-step instructions and embedded YouTube chef video tutorials.

🛒 INTEGRATED SMART SHOPPING LIST
• Seamlessly convert low-stock pantry items into your active shopping list with one tap.
• Group shopping items by category and tick them off while at the supermarket.
• Instantly convert bought shopping items into your pantry inventory!

📊 CHEF STATS & WASTE PREVENTION ANALYTICS
• Track your Zero-Waste Streak and see total rescued food items.
• Monitor estimated money saved and CO2 emissions avoided.
• Review monthly household grocery spending and identify primary waste causes.

💾 TOTAL PRIVACY & OFFLINE-FIRST RELIABILITY
• 100% Offline-First: All pantry data is securely stored on your private device using a local SQLite database.
• No Mandatory Accounts: Start using all core features immediately without sharing an email address.
• Optional Admin Sync: Connect to your self-hosted local Home Pantry Web Admin Console whenever you want.
• Full Data Export: Export your grocery records to CSV or create complete JSON database backups anytime.
